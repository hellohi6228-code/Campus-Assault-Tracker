    Data & naming policy

    • Sourced only. Every case cites published reporting, court records, or government findings. Nothing is included from rumor or social media.
    • Survivors are never named, even when they have chosen to go public.
    • Individuals are named only if convicted in criminal court, or if deceased and the subject of criminal charges or a university-commissioned investigation. People who are only accused — in a lawsuit, a Title IX process, or the press — are not named, and no photos of individuals are published.
    • Why: accusations are sometimes wrong (see Duke and UVA below), a public name-and-face list can't be taken back, and publishing unproven accusations exposes this site and its maintainers to defamation liability. Holding institutions accountable is what this tracker focuses on.
    • Not a prevalence measure. Big numbers here reflect big, well-reported scandals — not which campus is most dangerous. For per-campus reported-offense counts, see the Department of Education's Campus Safety and Security (Clery) data.
    • Found an error or a documented case that's missing? Open an issue or pull request on the project's GitHub repository with sources.
